What to ask before you pay

  1. Is the diagnostic fee credited if I approve the repair today?
  2. Will I get a written estimate and approve parts before any work starts?
  3. What is the parts-and-labor warranty — and is it in writing?
  4. Do you provide a COI if my building requires one?
  5. Do you service my brand and model (including sealed-system work if needed)?
