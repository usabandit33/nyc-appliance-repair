Quick answer: How much does appliance repair cost in NYC?

Appliance repair in New York City usually starts with a diagnostic or service-call fee, often roughly $80–$175, before any repair work begins. Completed jobs commonly fall in the $150–$500 range depending on the appliance, parts, and labor. Some shops credit the diagnostic fee if you approve the repair on the same visit; others do not. Always ask for the fee, credit rule, and a written estimate before authorizing parts or labor.

NYC appliance repair checklist

  1. What is the diagnostic / service-call fee? Common range in New York City is roughly $80–$175. Ask if it is credited or waived only if you approve the repair.
  2. Will I get a written estimate before any work starts? Require authorization for parts and labor. No verbal “around X” that changes after the tech is inside.
  3. What is the warranty on parts and labor? Typical independent shops offer 30–180 days; some go to 12 months. Get it in writing.
  4. What is the arrival window and same-day availability? Borough logistics matter. Confirm COI if your building requires a certificate of insurance.
  5. Which brands and appliances do they actually service? Some shops decline older units or specific sealed-system work. Ask about your model.
